About Showtime

Mountain roots. A family name. A craft built over generations.

Showtime began in 1986 as a specialty coach and custom-vehicle business and grew into the collision repair facility mountain drivers know today.

Our story

From custom coaches to collision repair.

Third-generation automotive craftsman Mark Makeig and his wife Lisa, together with Gilbert Nava and their parents Armando and Martha Nava, established Showtime Custom Coach, Inc. in October 1986.

Early work included limousine manufacturing, specialty vehicle construction, automotive furnishings, and vehicle armoring. Over time, the company’s craftsmanship and problem-solving evolved into complex collision repair for the San Bernardino mountain communities and surrounding Inland Empire.

Today, the next generation continues that family operation with a focus on technical training, modern repair capability, customer communication, and work that the team is willing to stand behind.
