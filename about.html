Our Story

The Showtime Standard

Showtime Collision, also known as Showtime Custom Coach, is a collision repair and auto body shop serving the San Bernardino mountains and Inland Empire from a main facility in Running Springs, with a second location serving Big Bear.

Showtime Collision is a collision repair and auto body shop serving Running Springs, Big Bear, Lake Arrowhead, Crestline, and the surrounding mountain and Inland Empire communities. Showtime specializes in collision repair, auto body repair, auto paint repair, bumper repair, dent and scratch repair, and insurance claim repair assistance — helping drivers restore their vehicles with clear communication, quality craftsmanship, and a finish worth showing off.

What does “Showtime condition” mean?

Showtime condition means a repair that is correct underneath and clean on the surface — aligned body lines, matched paint, proper panel fit, and a final finish worth showing off. It is the standard we work to on every vehicle.
