Privacy Policy.

What information Showtime collects through this website and how it is used.

Last updated: January 2025

Showtime Collision (Showtime Custom Coach) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you submit an estimate request or contact Showtime, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email, vehicle details, a description of the damage, any photos you upload, and any insurance claim information you choose to share.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to respond to your request, prepare a preliminary estimate, coordinate your repair, and communicate with you about your vehicle by phone, text, or email when you have agreed to be contacted.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share repair-related information with your insurer or parts suppliers only as needed to complete your repair.

Data Retention & Security

We keep estimate and repair records as needed to provide our services and meet business and legal requirements, and we take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us.

Your Choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time, and you can request access to or deletion of the information you have submitted by contacting Showtime.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies. Some are strictly necessary to run the site (for example, remembering your cookie choice) and are always on. Others, such as analytics cookies that help us understand site traffic, are only set after you accept them in the cookie banner shown on your first visit.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Preferences button in the footer of any page. California residents have additional rights regarding personal information under the CCPA/CPRA; contact us using the details below to make a request.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call Showtime at 909.867.7025.
