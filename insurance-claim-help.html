First Steps

What to Do After an Accident

If anyone is injured or the vehicle is unsafe, prioritize safety first. Once everyone is safe, a few simple steps make the repair process smoother.

  • Make sure everyone is safe
  • Document the scene with photos
  • Exchange information
  • Contact your insurer
  • Keep your claim and estimate
  • Call Showtime with questions
What to Send

What to Send Showtime

The more we can see, the more clearly we can explain your options. If you already have an insurance estimate, include it.

  • Photos of all four sides
  • Close-ups of the damage
  • A wide photo showing location
  • Your insurance estimate, if available
  • Claim number, if you have one
  • Vehicle year, make & model
Hidden Damage

Why Insurance Estimates May Change

An initial insurance estimate is based on visible damage. Some damage is only found after inspection or disassembly, which is why estimates can change.

  • Initial estimates use visible damage
  • Hidden damage is found on inspection
  • Disassembly can reveal more
  • Supplements may be needed
  • We explain each step
  • No surprises before approval
