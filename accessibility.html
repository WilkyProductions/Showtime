Last updated: January 2025

Showtime Collision is committed to making this website usable for as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

Our Approach

We aim to follow widely recognized accessibility practices, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA where reasonably achievable. This includes:

  • Readable color contrast for text and interface elements.
  • Descriptive alternative text on meaningful images.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and forms.
  • Clear, labeled form fields on our estimate and contact forms.
  • Responsive layouts that work on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Ongoing Work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and some content may not yet be fully accessible. We continue to review and improve the site over time.

Need Help or Found a Problem?

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or you would like information in another format, call Showtime at 909.867.7025 and we will do our best to help.
