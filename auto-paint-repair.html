Auto Paint Repair

Paint is where a repair either disappears or stands out. Showtime focuses on paint match, gloss, and blending so refinished panels look like they belong on your vehicle.

How does auto paint matching work?

Paint matching starts with identifying the vehicle’s color and condition, then mixing and blending the refinish so the repaired panel matches the surrounding panels in color and gloss. Showtime refinishes in a dedicated paint booth for a clean, controlled result.
