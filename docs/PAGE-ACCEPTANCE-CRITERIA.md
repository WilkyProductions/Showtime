# Page Acceptance Criteria

## Home

- Primary Carwise CTA visible without scrolling on common mobile and desktop sizes.
- Phone CTA visible in hero and mobile action bar.
- Trust signals appear near the hero.
- Three customer-intent paths remain easy to scan.
- OEM section names the approved roster without mixing in exotics.
- Real repair proof and facility proof appear before the final CTA.

## Locations hub

- Separates physical facilities from service areas.
- Running Springs and Big Bear have distinct roles.
- Lake Arrowhead and Crestline do not imply staffed storefronts.
- Facility tour section explains equipment in customer language.
- Avoids claiming certification based only on signs visible in photographs.
- Facility tour includes arrival/check-in, structural, paint, and quality-control areas.

## Running Springs

- Complete address, hours, phone, directions, and facility role above the fold.
- Clearly positioned as the complete repair/production campus.

## Big Bear

- Uses “staffed satellite,” not “full repair shop,” unless the operating model changes.
- States intake capability, staff assistance, rental cars on site, and Running Springs production coordination.
- Uses the confirmed Big Bear map/address before launch.

## Lake Arrowhead / Crestline

- Contains community-specific logistics and proof.
- No identical city-name-swapped copy.
- Primary CTA remains the Carwise estimate.

## OEM Certifications

- Dedicated only to specific OEM credentials and covered makes.
- Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, and Cadillac are visible under General Motors.
- I-CAR is described separately.
- Big Bear and Running Springs roles remain distinct.
- No exotic capability claims appear on this page.
- Official badges are used only after verification and approval.

## Results

- Before/after controls work with mouse, touch, and keyboard.
- Each result includes factual repair context.
- Future exotic stories use project-specific media and do not imply OEM certification.

## Insurance

- Explains customer choice, estimate differences, supplements, and next steps without promising claim outcomes.
- Sends photo-estimate users to Carwise.

## Contact

- Complete, consistent location information.
- No generic form inserted ahead of the estimate funnel.
