What we repair

Collision & structural repair

Damage analysis, disassembly planning, structural measuring, panel replacement, frame or unibody work, and final quality checks.

Auto body & panel repair

Repair or replacement of damaged body panels with careful attention to gaps, body lines, corrosion protection, and fit.

Paint matching & refinishing

PPG color matching, panel blending when required, paint preparation, controlled refinishing, and finish inspection.

Bumper & safety-system considerations

Bumper covers, brackets, reinforcements, sensors, lamps, and related components are evaluated as part of the repair plan.

Dent, scratch & cosmetic repair

Solutions for surface damage, dents, scrapes, scuffs, and localized refinishing when the damage is an appropriate candidate.

Suspension, alignment & diagnostics

Collision-related suspension diagnosis, wheel alignment, scan-related checks, and repair planning for affected vehicle systems.

Automotive glass replacement

Windshield and automotive glass replacement with attention to related moldings, cameras, sensors, and calibration needs.

Insurance claim repair support

Estimate review, documentation, repair-plan explanation, supplement coordination, and direct communication during the claim process.
