What customers usually want to know.

Clear distinctions prevent a certification page from becoming a wall of unexplained logos.

Which makes are currently shown as OEM certified?

Subaru, Ford, Honda, Acura, Nissan, INFINITI, Hyundai, Kia, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, FIAT, Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, and Cadillac.

Can Showtime repair makes that are not on this list?

Potentially, yes. Repair capability and OEM certification are different claims. Vehicles outside this roster are evaluated based on damage, procedures, parts, equipment, and project fit, but are not represented as certified on this page.

Is I-CAR Gold Class another OEM certification?

No. I-CAR Gold Class is a separate industry training credential. It supports technician education but should not be counted as another manufacturer certification.

Can I begin an OEM-certified repair through Big Bear?

Yes. The staffed Big Bear satellite can begin intake and help coordinate the process. Certified production repairs are performed through the Running Springs facility.
