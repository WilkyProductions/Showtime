Insurance Claims

Already Have an Insurance Claim?

Insurance repair estimates can be confusing, and some damage is not visible until the vehicle is inspected. Showtime can help you understand the process and what happens next.

  • Bring or upload your estimate. Send your insurance estimate with photos of the damage.
  • Understand hidden damage. Some damage is only found after inspection or disassembly. We’ll explain what can and cannot be confirmed from photos alone.
  • Know the repair plan. We help you understand the repair process before the work begins.

Does Showtime help with insurance claims?

Yes. If you already have an insurance estimate or claim information, Showtime can review the details, explain the repair process, and help you understand the next step. Have questions about your repair options? Contact Showtime and we’ll help you understand the next step.
