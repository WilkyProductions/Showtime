Clear answers reduce collision-repair stress.

These are the questions customers usually ask before submitting photos or visiting the shop.

Call the Shop
Can I receive a final repair price from photos alone?

Photos are useful for an initial review, but hidden damage, scans, measurements, and disassembly may be needed before the final repair plan is known.

Can Showtime work with my insurance company?

Yes we work with all insurance companies. You have the right to choose your repair facility.

What should I prepare for the online estimate?

Use a mobile phone, have the vehicle accessible, and be ready to capture the guided photos requested by Carwise.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

Calling ahead or starting the photo estimate is the smoothest path. Appointments are available upon request, and walk-in availability depends on the shop schedule.

Which vehicle makes is Showtime OEM certified to repair?

We are currently certified in Subaru, Ford, Honda, Acura, Nissan, Infiniti, Hyundai, Kia, Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, Fiat, Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, and Cadillac. Certification applies to the Running Springs production facility.

Where can I start in person?

Full collision repair is performed at 32049 Encina Way, Running Springs, CA 92382. Big Bear also has a staffed satellite facility where customers can begin intake, get the process started, and arrange an on-site rental car.
