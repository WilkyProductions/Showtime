Take a tour of our entire facility.

A closer look at the Running Springs shop, from the front office and guest lobby to the full tour of the shop facilities. Our guest lobby has been artfully designed to embody the passion, craftsmanship, and attention to detail that is invested into every repair. Filled with automotive furnishings and vintage decor built by Mark and his team over the years, it creates a remarkably enjoyable environment for every guest. An immaculate, organized facility is crucial to producing high-quality collision repairs. Eliminating clutter and unnecessary tools increases efficiency and steady flow through the repair process from start to finish. Our team takes pride in maintaining exceptionally clean work areas guaranteed not to be found anywhere else in the industry.

In addition to exceptionally clean work areas, we also expanded to include an isolated OEM-required aluminum “clean room” that keeps aluminum and steel repairs separated to prevent corrosion and cross-contamination.

Designated Aluminum Clean Room, Hand Tools & Welders

As vehicle technology advances and aluminum use increases across many makes, our facility includes an isolated aluminum clean room with dedicated aluminum welders and hand tools, preventing galvanic corrosion from cross-contamination with steel repairs. Required by many manufacturers for OEM certification, we maintain the proper facilities, equipment, and Aluminum Welding Certified technicians to handle aluminum repairs and replacements per OEM guidelines.

Precision equipment & technology.

Beyond paint and body work, the Running Springs facility runs OEM-grade alignment, welding, tire, and climate-control equipment in house.

Hunter RX10 Scissor Lift

All collision-related alignments are performed on-site on this top-of-the-line alignment lift, built for maximum efficiency and productivity.

Ravaglioli Alignment Machine

A laser box is projected around the vehicle to build a 3-D image, used to measure suspension geometry and confirm the vehicle is restored to pre-accident specifications.

Car-O-Liner Vision X3

A robotic arm on a track uses precise 3D measuring technology to detect structural misalignment down to the millimeter and build a repair plan back to pre-accident condition.

Pro-Spot i5 Smart Welder

Liquid-cooled tips, 1,243 pounds of electrode force, and auto-weld technology keep squeeze-type resistance spot welding on the cutting edge, so every weld is safe and correct.

Pro-Spot SP-2

A synergic, pulse-technology MIG welder that welds every type of steel and uses silicone bronze MIG brazing. Our I-CAR welding certified technicians work within factory specifications.

Nitrocell Plastic Welding

With plastic parts often exceeding $2,000 to replace, this system uses pure heated nitrogen to melt plastic and filler rod together, eliminating oxidation for a strong, quality weld.

Tire Mounting & Balancing

A Hunter TC39 mounts tires on wheels of every type, and a Hunter RFE Road Force Elite balances to exact spec while detecting whether a tire was damaged in the collision.

A/C Service Equipment

Robinair and Snap-On equipment, including 1234YF refrigerant capability, lets our EPA-certified technicians service any air conditioning system affected by a collision.

PPG Computerized OEM Color-Matching

Computerized and precisely-measured, our PPG mixing room is equipped with everything necessary to create the perfect color formula and match for every vehicle. Using only the highest quality PPG Envirobase line of paint, our expert refinish team is highly-trained in restoring your vehicle's paint back to pre-accident condition.

Spraytech Premier Downdraft Spray Booth

With filtered fresh air flow and exhaust filters, the heated spray booth allows for OE-quality refinishes, no matter the weather conditions. Temperature-controlled conditions ensure clean refinishes year-round for all makes and models.
