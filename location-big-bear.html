Is Big Bear a full collision repair shop?

Big Bear is a staffed satellite intake facility. The team can get the process started, collect claim and vehicle information, and help arrange a rental car. Complete repair operations are performed at the Running Springs facility.

Can I get a rental car at the Big Bear location?

Rental cars are available on site, subject to current availability and rental requirements. Call ahead so the team can help confirm the best plan.

What should I bring to the satellite facility?

Bring your driver’s license, insurance information, claim number, insurer estimate if you have one, and the vehicle when it is safe to drive.

Can I begin without visiting in person?

Yes. Use the Carwise photo-estimate flow on a mobile phone, then the team can help determine whether the next step should be the Big Bear satellite or the Running Springs facility.

What if the vehicle is not safe to drive?

Do not drive a vehicle with safety concerns. Call Showtime so the team can discuss vehicle movement and the safest intake plan.
