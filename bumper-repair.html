Bumper Repair

A scuffed or cracked bumper is often more than cosmetic. Showtime inspects the cover and the structure behind it, including sensors and brackets, before recommending repair or replacement.

Should a bumper be repaired or replaced?

It depends on the damage. Minor scrapes and cracks can often be repaired and refinished, while heavier damage or damage to sensors, brackets, and mounting points may require replacement. Showtime inspects the bumper and recommends the right repair path.

Repair Paths

Repair vs. Replace

Not every bumper needs to be replaced. We inspect the damage, including the structure behind the cover, before recommending a repair path.

  • Surface scrapes & scuffs
  • Cracks & splits
  • Dents & deformation
  • Mounting & bracket damage
  • Sensor & camera areas
  • Paint & finish damage
Inspection

Hidden Damage Behind the Bumper

Some bumpers include sensors, brackets, and safety-related components that may need inspection after impact, even when the outside looks minor.

  • Reinforcement bars
  • Mounting brackets
  • Parking & impact sensors
  • Camera systems
  • Energy absorbers
  • Wiring & connectors
Finish

Paint Match on Bumpers

Bumpers are made of different materials than body panels, which can affect how paint behaves. We focus on matching color and gloss to the surrounding panels.

  • Material-correct prep
  • Flexible primers
  • Color match
  • Gloss match
  • Clean blending
  • Final inspection
